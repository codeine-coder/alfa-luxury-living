Before you move

Renting at Alfa Luxury Living.

What apartment layouts can I rent?

Choose from one-bedroom and two-bedroom apartments, including two-bedroom layouts with an office or two bathrooms. View floor plans and starting rents, and contact our leasing consultant to confirm availability.

What comes with my rental?

Each apartment includes an in-unit washer and dryer, dishwasher, gas stove, updated stainless steel appliances, and marble/granite countertops. One parking space is included. Utilities are separate, and costs vary by residence.

How do I inquire about an apartment?

Use the rental inquiry form to choose a floor plan and prepare an email to Elvin Ruiz, or call 973-288-3384. Our leasing consultant can answer questions about rent, availability, and next steps.
